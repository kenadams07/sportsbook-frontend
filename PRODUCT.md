# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are internal sportsbook operations staff: traders and risk managers, customer support agents, and finance or settlement teams. Whitelabel/client managers may have limited access through role-based permissions, but the product is optimized for internal operational workflows.

## Product Purpose

This admin frontend helps sportsbook teams manage events, odds, users, bets, markets, results, reports, and operational settings. Success means staff can complete high-stakes tasks quickly, understand system state clearly, and trust that critical actions, alerts, and errors are visible and precise.

## Brand Personality

Secure, precise, efficient. The interface should feel trustworthy and operational rather than promotional, with restrained visual choices that support speed, accuracy, and confidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer sportsbook styling, decorative betting-site energy, heavy gradients, oversized marketing panels, playful motion, vague empty states, weak validation, and low-contrast alerts. Do not prioritize visual drama over task clarity.

## Design Principles

- Optimize for operational focus: each screen should make the primary task and system state immediately clear.
- Make risk visible: errors, alerts, disabled states, and destructive actions need strong hierarchy and unambiguous language.
- Prefer familiar admin patterns: use consistent forms, tables, filters, navigation, and feedback instead of invented controls.
- Support fast repeat use: keyboard flows, predictable tab order, compact hierarchy, and low-friction recovery matter more than decorative flourish.
- Preserve trust through consistency: spacing, controls, labels, and state styling should feel like one product vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the minimum standard. Support keyboard-first workflows, clear visible focus states, full screen-reader semantics, reduced-motion preferences, high contrast for critical actions and alerts, touch targets of at least 44px where applicable, and consistent tab order.
